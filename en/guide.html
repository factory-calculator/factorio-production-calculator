How to use the calculator

Learn how to set production targets, choose machines, and understand your results.

Getting started

  1. Choose the base game or Space Age calculator from the home page.
  2. Select the item or recipe you want to produce.
  3. Enter your target production rate and choose a time unit.
  4. Review the required materials, machines, and production flow.
  5. Add more products to the production plan if you want to plan several goals together.
Results depend on the selected recipes and game settings. Adjust machines and material supply to match your factory.

Base game mode

This mode is intended for recipes and materials available in the standard Factorio game. It calculates the machines and material flow required for production.

  • Select recipes available in the base game.
  • Enter a target rate and review the required materials and machines.
  • Space Age-only planetary and space-related recipes are excluded.

Space Age mode

Use this mode when your game includes the Space Age expansion. It covers expansion-related production content and conditions supported by the calculator.

  • Select recipes and production conditions added by Space Age.
  • If planetary conditions are shown, choose the environment where you plan to produce.
  • The base game and Space Age are separate calculator modes.
Do not use Space Age results directly for a base-game factory plan. Recipes and production conditions may differ.

Machines & modules

Production machines

Machine type and speed affect the number of machines required. Treat the result as a planning estimate and also consider space and transport capacity.

Modules & beacons

Where module or beacon settings are available, select the same configuration used in your game. Recheck machine requirements and material consumption after changing settings.

Fractional machine counts

Fractional values represent required production capacity. In practice, you will usually need to round up to a whole machine.

Understanding the results

  • Target rate: the amount of an item you want to produce per selected time unit.
  • Required materials: the materials consumed to meet the target.
  • Machines: an estimate of the production capacity or machine count required under the selected settings.
  • Production rate: the output speed based on the selected machines and recipes.

Use the same time unit when comparing values expressed per second, minute, or another interval.

Frequently asked questions

Is my production plan saved automatically?

Item names and target rates added to the production plan are saved in your current browser. Base game and Space Age plans are saved separately. Data is not synced across browsers or devices, and clearing site data may remove it.

What is the difference between the two modes?

The base game mode covers the standard Factorio game. Space Age also includes supported recipes and production conditions added by the expansion.

Are Space Age recipes included in the base game mode?

No. The recipe datasets and calculation modes are kept separate.

Why are machine counts sometimes fractional?

Fractional values show the required production capacity. Round up or adjust the number of machines to fit your factory layout.

Why does the result differ slightly from the game?

Check that the recipe, machine, modules, beacons, research, and game version match your in-game setup.

Can I use the calculator on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Open the site in a modern browser. Landscape orientation may help on narrower screens.

How can I report a bug?

Record the edition, selected item, target rate, and what happened. This information makes it easier to investigate.
