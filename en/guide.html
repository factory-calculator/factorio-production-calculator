GUIDE & FAQ

How to use the calculator

A quick guide to setting production targets and reading the results.

Getting started

  1. Open the home page and choose Factorio or Space Age.
  2. Select the item or recipe you want to produce.
  3. Enter your target production rate and choose the time unit.
  4. Review the required ingredients, machine requirements, and production flow shown by the calculator.
  5. Add more products if you want to plan several production goals together.
Important: Choose the edition that matches your game. Space Age recipes and conditions should not be mixed into a base-game-only production plan.

Base game mode

The base game mode is intended for the recipes and materials available in the standard Factorio game. Space Age-only recipes and planetary production conditions are kept out of this mode.

Space Age mode

Use this mode when your factory uses the Space Age expansion. It includes expansion-related production content and conditions where supported by the calculator.

Understanding the results

  • Target rate: the amount of an item you want to produce per selected time unit.
  • Ingredients: the materials consumed to meet the target.
  • Machines: an estimate of the production capacity or number of machines required under the selected settings.
  • Fractional machine counts: these represent required capacity; the number of machines you place in-game may need to be rounded up.

Common questions

Why does my result differ from the game?

Check the recipe, machine, modules, beacons, research, and game version. A difference in any of these conditions can change the result.

Can I use it on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Open the site in a modern browser. If the layout feels cramped, try landscape orientation.

Where can I report a bug?

Use the feedback form linked from the Japanese home page, and include the edition, selected recipe, target rate, and what you expected to happen.
